Verification outcome and rework — Desk

L0 data contract · surface: Desk (internal ops console) · products/desk/verification-outcome-and-rework · v1 · status: draft

Feature: verification-outcome-and-rework · Surface: Desk — verification officer, with the support agent and compliance officer views where the role changes the screen · Outcome: a rework item lands in the queue with its reason code, the field that differs and the rule that fired; a provider failure shows as PROVIDER-FAILED and never as a blank or a decline; every regulated action on the case is an audit row · Context: context/ last verified 2026-11-02; D-004 and D-006 PENDING; D-002 LOCKED (queue order).

Drawn against: context/surfaces/desk/current-behaviour.md rows DESK-001 to DESK-006, DESK-009, DESK-010 (2026-11-02). No production screenshot is in this repo; the shell is derived from those rows. The feature extends DESK-004 (field that differs) and fills the queue gap for cases with more than one open item.

Greyscale wireframe. Fictional data. Fixtures are labelled examples, not verified calculations, approved policy or working integrations.

Chip: [ POINT-ID · unit/type · provenance · current storage · disposition ] — every chip resolves to a row in ../internal/data-packet.csv. Dispositions: reuse · extend · proposed new · configure · derive · unresolved. Strips: EMPTY · PARTIAL · MISSING · RESTRICTED · PROVIDER-FAILED · REWORK — meanings in tools/wireframe-kit/kit.md.

Copy: Desk ships in English. The customer-facing text is shown as an English preview; the message goes out in the customer's language.
