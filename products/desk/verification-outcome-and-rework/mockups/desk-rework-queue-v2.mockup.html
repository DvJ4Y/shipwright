Verification outcome and rework — Desk

L0 data contract · surface: Desk (internal ops console) · products/desk/verification-outcome-and-rework · v2 · status: draft · screenshot check: not run · variation axis: Data Field

Feature: verification-outcome-and-rework · Surface: Desk — verification officer, with the support agent and compliance officer views where the role changes the screen · Outcome: the officer raises one item the customer can act on, sees a multi-item case as one row with counts that agree, and records a manual verification with its method and result after the retry limit; a provider failure is a row flag and never a decline · Context: drawn against shared/prd.md v1.1 (§4, R1–R14, §7, §8, A1–A16); context/ last verified 2026-11-02.

Drawn against: context/surfaces/desk/current-behaviour.md rows DESK-001 to DESK-006, DESK-009, DESK-010 (2026-11-02). No production screenshot is in this repo; the shell is derived from those rows. Changed from v1: the row shows open items and how many await re-check (R10), the clock as stopped or paused, and the hold date; "not recorded" replaces MISSING text in cells; the return panel carries the requirement, the accepted alternatives and the round; the officer's note is internal and no longer chipped as customer text; a re-check block shows the take-another-look request and a corrected detail; PROVIDER-FAILED is a row flag with the sub-status unchanged (open, or rework with items open); attempts "N of 3" (two retries, example); "Retry now" removed; the manual method defaults to document inspection with video call and field visit shown as open inputs; the result is verified or not matched; the consent record is for the compliance officer only; the delete-after date for the compliance officer and the ops lead.

Greyscale wireframe. Fictional data. Fixtures are labelled examples, not verified calculations, approved policy or working integrations.

Chip: [ POINT-ID · unit/type · provenance · current storage · disposition ] — every chip resolves to a row in internal/data-packet.csv. Strips: EMPTY · PARTIAL · MISSING · RESTRICTED · PROVIDER-FAILED · REWORK — meanings in tools/wireframe-kit/kit.md.

Copy: Desk ships in English. The customer-facing text is shown as an English preview; the message goes out in the case language from templates only (R2).
