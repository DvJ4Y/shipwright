Feature: verification-outcome-and-rework · Surface: Harbor · Outcome: a returned document is fixed once — the customer sees which paper, which detail differs, the requirement in plain words, the accepted alternatives, the recovery paths and the send-by window per item; a provider failure is never a decline · Context: drawn against shared/prd.md v1.1 (§4, R1–R14, §7, §8, A1–A16); context/ last verified 2026-11-02.

Drawn against: context/surfaces/harbor/current-behaviour.md rows HUB-003, HUB-004, HUB-005, HUB-006, HUB-007, HUB-008, HUB-009 (2026-11-02). No production screenshot is in this repo; the shell is derived from those rows. Changed from v1: per-item recovery paths per R4 (correct the detail, ask our team to take another look); "talk to our team" on every message; accepted alternatives and the requirement in plain words on every item; the send-by window per item, never "Not shown" on a returned item; new frames S2 (second return), S3 (correct a detail, with the premium warning), S4 (visit being arranged), S5 (on hold), S6 (lapsed); the missing-template case; PROVIDER-FAILED with no send-by field when nothing is returned, and combined with open items.

Greyscale wireframe. Fictional data. Fixtures are labelled examples, not verified calculations, approved policy or working integrations.

Chip: [ POINT-ID · unit/type · provenance · current storage · disposition ] — every chip resolves to a row in internal/data-packet.csv. Strips: EMPTY · PARTIAL · MISSING · RESTRICTED · PROVIDER-FAILED · REWORK — meanings in tools/wireframe-kit/kit.md. The PRD states ON HOLD and LAPSED as states; they sit under the REWORK strip on S5 and S6 because an item stays open.

Copy: English, with [customer language] on every localised string (HUB-003). Strings are written for a relative who may read them. The customer never sees the risk tier, the configured rule name, an internal id or the identity number. WhatsApp carries one line per document plus the link to this page (R2).
