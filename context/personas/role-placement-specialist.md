## Who

One of two specialists (`example`) who take a `pass` case, choose the insurer whose appetite
fits, submit it, answer insurer queries and see payment through (E1). Works the two
email-based partners by hand (E1, E2).

## Jobs

- When a case passes verification, the specialist wants the appetite notes for all six
  partners beside the case, so the first choice is the right one. (E1)
- When an insurer asks a question, the specialist wants to answer from the verified record,
  so the customer is not called. (E1)
- When payment is pending, the specialist wants to see whether the link was opened, so the
  follow-up is timed. (E1)

## Pain points

- Appetite changes arrive by email and are not in Desk. (E1)
- Two partners' acceptances are recorded by hand and lag the figure (`PLC-M1`). (E1, E2)

## What we don't know

- How often an insurer query is really a verification gap — ask; it changes who owns the fix.
- Whether the specialist or the customer should choose between two acceptable insurers —
  a product decision with no owner yet.
