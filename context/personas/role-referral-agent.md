## Who

One of about 120 (`example`) local people — shopkeepers, accountants, a hardware supplier —
who refer shop owners they already know (E1). Paid commission on issued policies; checks
status in Field (E1, E2). Not an employee; no training material exists (E3).

## Jobs

- When a shop owner asks, the agent wants to add a referral with a name and number, so the
  referral is credited before the owner changes their mind. (E1)
- When a case stalls, the agent wants to know it is `on hold` and why, so they can nudge the
  owner. (E1, E2)
- When a policy is issued, the agent wants to see the commission and when it pays, so they
  trust the arrangement. (E1)

## Pain points

- Learning a case is stuck only when the owner complains. (E1, E2)
- Not knowing what a rework reason means well enough to explain it. (E1, E3)
- Commission visible only after a call to the ops lead. (E1, E3)

## Signals

| Dimension | Reality | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Channel | Field on a phone; WhatsApp with the shop owner | E1 |
| Language | Their own; Field is in English and is used for status, not reading | E1 |
| Motivation | Commission, and standing with the shop owners they refer | E1 |

## What we don't know

- Whether the agent should see the rework reason itself or only "a document is needed" —
  a data-handling decision for the compliance officer.
- The share of referred cases that would have come anyway — not measurable today.
