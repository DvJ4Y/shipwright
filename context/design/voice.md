# Brand voice — for product copy

> Last verified: 2026-11-02

Plain. Dignified. No blame. Every refusal carries a next step. Write in the customer's
language first; English is one option among several.

## The four rules

| Rule | Means | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plain** | Everyday words; one idea per sentence; the number before the explanation | "Your premium is between ₹4,200 and ₹5,100 a year." (`example`) | "Indicative premium subject to underwriting." |
| **Dignified** | The customer is a business owner making a decision, not an applicant being judged | "Your shop fits our standard cover." | "You qualify." / "You have been approved." |
| **No blame** | A problem is a thing to fix, not a fault to assign | "We could not read the shop licence photo. Try it in daylight, flat on a table." | "You uploaded an invalid document." |
| **A next step on every refusal** | Declined, rework, on hold and provider-failed each end with what to do and where | "This cover is not available for an open yard above ₹1 crore (`example`). You can lower the stock amount, or ask our team to review." | "Your application has been declined." |

## Words we use and words we do not

| Say | Not | Why |
|---|---|---|
| your policy, your cover | the product, the plan | It is theirs |
| returned, needs a new photo | rejected, failed, invalid | A document is fixable |
| our team will take a look | under review, escalated | A person, in plain words |
| not available for … right now | ineligible, does not qualify | Describes the cover, not the person |
| we could not complete the identity check | verification failed | The provider failed, not the customer |
| within one working day | SLA, TAT, a bare hour count | A commitment, not a code |

## Never

- Never say or imply an insurer rejected the customer. The rules did not fit the
  information; the information can change.
- Never show a score, a tier number or a risk word to a customer.
- Never ask for something the customer has already given. If it must be asked again, say
  why.
- Never use urgency to close a sale. State the fact and the choice.

## Per audience

| Audience | Tone shift |
|---|---|
| Customer (Harbor, WhatsApp) | Warm, short, in their language; one action per message |
| Referral agent (Field) | Direct, status-first; "a customer action is pending" without the reason |
| Staff (Desk, Field verifier) | Neutral, dense, English; the rule name and the field, no softening |
| Insurer partner | Formal, complete; the verified record speaks |

## Message shape

Every customer message has four parts in this order: what happened · what it means for the
policy · what to do next · where to do it. A message missing part three is not sent.

## Review check

Before a string ships: read it aloud as the customer; find the next step; find any word from
the "Not" column; check the language variant was written, not translated.
